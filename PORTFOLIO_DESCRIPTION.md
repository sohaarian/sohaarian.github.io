# Workers’ Compensation Claims Analytics

Developed a local claims-intelligence product using official New York State Workers’ Compensation Board records and FRED Medical Care CPI data. The application generates animated executive reporting, analyzes claim outcomes and adjudication patterns, surfaces explainable litigation-review indicators, and provides claim-level source evidence. It also benchmarks a local evidence agent that plans searches and follows claim identifiers against a fixed-chunk RAG baseline, reporting accuracy, latency, estimated hosted cost, traceability, and a ship recommendation.

The public-data product measures injury outcomes, attorney representation, controversy, hearings, appeals, carrier patterns, geography, timelines, and medical-price context. Billing behavior and reserve development are supported as schema-ready extensions for authorized de-identified carrier data and are not fabricated from public records.
